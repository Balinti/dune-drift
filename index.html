Dune Drift — Free Browser HTML5 Game

Dune Drift is a free hyper-casual HTML5 canvas game. You control a sand surfer riding the glowing crest of ever-shifting desert dunes. Tap and hold to carve tight arcs; release to glide at speed. Thread through Wind Gates to stack your combo multiplier. Dodge rocks lurking off the crest for near-miss bonuses. The colour palette shifts from warm amber to cool indigo as your combo grows. Features: combo multiplier, Wind Gates, near-miss bonuses, crosswind events telegraphed by sand streaks, hit-stop on perfect gate centres, particle sand spray, screen shake, high-DPI canvas, and local best-score persistence.
