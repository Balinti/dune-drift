About Dune Drift

Dune Drift is a free hyper-casual HTML5 browser game. Pilot a sand skimmer through an endless desert storm, dodging falling lane-walls. Graze wall edges to build your combo multiplier and chase the highest score possible on the shifting dune field.

How to Play

  • Tap / Space / Enter — Drift-dash to the opposite lane
  • Graze a storm wall edge to extend your combo streak and earn bonus points
  • Higher combo = higher multiplier = more points per second
  • Missing a graze resets your combo but the run continues
  • A direct hit ends the run — avoid solid walls!

The dune field drifts left and right over time, and after 60 seconds occasional sand gusts push lanes sideways. Stay sharp, keep combos going, and share your best multiplier with friends!
